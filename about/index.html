Our Story

About Dirty Paws

Dirty Paws Animal Rescue is a non-profit animal rescue organization located in Contra Costa County, here to provide services to the East Bay.

Our Mission

A Better Life for Cats

Dirty Paws Animal Rescue exists to provide a better life for adoptable companion animals and to assist the public in the effort to limit the overpopulation of these animals through spay & neuter programs.

  • We strive to provide better life opportunities for cats.
  • Dirty Paws Animal Rescue strives to take care of all of its animals, and ensure they are adopted to fantastic people.
