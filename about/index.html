About Us

Our mission, our story, and why we do this work

Who We Are

Dirty Paws Animal Rescue is an all-volunteer, non-profit rescue organization dedicated to providing a better life for adoptable companion animals in Contra Costa County. We rescue cats in need, find them loving homes, and work to reduce overpopulation through spay and neuter programs.

We believe every cat deserves a chance at a happy life. Whether it's a kitten found outside, a senior cat surrendered by a family, or a shy cat overlooked at a shelter — we take them in, care for them, and give them the best shot at finding their forever home.

What We Do

  • Rescue cats and kittens from shelters, the street, and difficult situations
  • Provide foster care, veterinary treatment, and socialization
  • Host adoption events at local PetSmart locations in Walnut Creek
  • Run spay and neuter programs to reduce cat overpopulation in Contra Costa County

Volunteer-Run

Every person at Dirty Paws Animal Rescue is a volunteer. No one draws a salary. Every dollar donated goes directly to the cats — food, medical care, supplies, and spay & neuter surgeries.

Our volunteers foster cats in their homes, transport animals to vet appointments, set up at adoption events, manage social media, and handle the day-to-day work of running a rescue. If you love cats and want to make a real difference, we'd love your help.
