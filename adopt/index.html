Good to Know

Adoption FAQ

How does the adoption process work?

Start by browsing our adoptable cats, then submit an adoption application. Once we review it, we will arrange a meet so you can spend time with the cat before finalizing the adoption.

What comes with my adopted cat?

Our cats are cared for through our spay, neuter, and rescue programs as part of our mission to give every cat a healthy start. We will go over each cat's specific medical history with you during the adoption process.

How long does adoption take?

Timelines vary depending on the cat and the application, but we work to keep the process quick and straightforward so the right cat can go home as soon as possible.

Is there an adoption fee?

Adoption fee details are coming soon. Please email us and we will be glad to walk you through the details.

What if the adoption is not the right fit?

We want every adoption to last a lifetime. If something is not working out, please reach out to us right away so we can help find the best outcome for you and the cat.

Can I adopt if I have other pets?

Many of our cats do well in homes with other animals. Tell us about your household in your application and we will help match you with a cat who is a good fit.
