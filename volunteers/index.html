A Two-Way Bond

Why Volunteer

What Volunteering Gives You

  • The joy of helping a cat find a forever home
  • A welcoming community of fellow animal lovers
  • Hands-on experience with rescue and adoption
  • The chance to make a real difference close to home

What You Bring

  • A few hours of your time when you can give them
  • Kindness and patience with the cats in our care
  • A helping hand at adoption events
  • Enthusiasm for our mission
Ways to Help

How Volunteers Pitch In

Volunteers help in many ways, from greeting visitors and showing cats at our adoption events to helping with transport and spreading the word on social media. Tell us a little about yourself and your availability, and we will find a role that fits.

Interested in fostering a cat? Our foster program details are coming soon — mention foster interest in your application or email us and we will follow up.
