Volunteer With Us

Dirty Paws runs entirely on volunteers — join our team

Why Volunteer?

Every person at Dirty Paws Animal Rescue is a volunteer. No paid staff. Every hour donated goes directly to improving the lives of cats in Contra Costa County. Volunteers are the heart of our rescue — without you, none of this happens.

Whether you can commit a few hours a month or a few hours a week, there's a role for you. We especially need fosters — cats in foster homes do far better than cats in kennels, and every foster spot saves a life.

Ways to Get Involved

Foster

Open your home to a cat or litter of kittens while they wait for adoption. We provide food, supplies, and vet care. You provide love and a safe space. This is our most critical need.

Transport

Drive cats to vet appointments, between foster homes, or to adoption events. Flexible scheduling — you pick the trips that work for you.

Adoption Events

Help set up, manage, and run adoption events at PetSmart and other locations. Great way to meet adopters and see cats find their homes in real time.

Admin & Social Media

Help with emails, social media posts, Petfinder listings, and other behind-the-scenes tasks that keep the rescue running.
