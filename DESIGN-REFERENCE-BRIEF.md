# Design Reference Brief — Street Cats Club (Kathleen's model site)
**Source: https://www.streetcatsclub.org (esp. /barncatprogram — the page Kathleen texted). Analyzed 2026-06-03.**
**Use: the build spec for the redesign on this branch. Full conversion patterns + Kathleen questions at bottom.**

## The aesthetic to replicate
- **Palette:** warm orange accents (#ff8044 primary, #f97425 deep) on cream/off-white backgrounds (#fef6ed, #fef3e5), white content areas, black body text. (Adapt warmth to Dirty Paws' rainbow-heart logo — cream/warm neutrals make the logo pop.)
- **Type:** bold ALL-CAPS condensed display headlines (Anton or similar Google Font) + clean modern sans body (Avenir-like: Inter/Nunito Sans). High contrast, warm, not cutesy.
- **Photos:** real candid cat photography, full-width strips + rectangular cards, minimal styling.
- **Density:** LOW. Generous white space. Distinct uncrowded sections.
- **Voice:** mission-forward, plain declarative, compassionate not sentimental. e.g. their tagline: "Not every cat can live indoors — but they still deserve a safe place to call home."

## Structural patterns to transplant (the heart of the brief)
1. **Numbered 3-step CTA on every program/adoption page** (their Barn Cat page: 1. Review Guide → 2. Fill Application → 3. Checklist). For DP adoption: 1. Meet our cats → 2. Submit application → 3. Schedule a meet.
2. **Reciprocal value framing** two-column "what you get / what you provide" on every program page (adoption, foster, volunteer).
3. **Impact stats row on homepage** (theirs: "2,426 cats fixed / 21,834 prevented"). DP numbers = NEEDS-KATHLEEN; build the component with placeholder labels.
4. **Persistent DONATE button in header, all pages** (DP → PayPal link from inventory).
5. **Dedicated Adoption FAQ page** (fees, timeline, what comes with the cat, process) — DRAFTABLE.
6. **Footer = full secondary nav hub** (4 columns: Adopt / Programs / Support / About+Forms).
7. **Consolidated Support/Donate page** (one-time + other ways to help together).
8. Team/Mission pages (NEEDS-KATHLEEN content; stub gracefully).

## Their IA (adapt, don't clone)
ADOPT (Adoptable Cats · Application · FAQs) · SERVICES/Programs · EVENTS · SUPPORT (Volunteer · Ways to Give · Wishlists) · ABOUT (Mission/History · Team · Contact · Newsletter) · DONATE (header CTA).

## Do NOT copy
Wix-specific widgets/mechanics · their fragmented 5-platform form stack (DP consolidates) · standalone event pages (DP = one events page) · merch shop (not Year 1) · their LACK of success stories (DP should have them — their gap, not a pattern).
