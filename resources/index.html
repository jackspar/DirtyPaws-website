Resources

A few helpful starting points for caring for cats in our community. We will keep adding to this page over time.

Spay & Neuter

Spaying and neutering is one of the most effective ways to reduce cat overpopulation, and it is central to our mission. If you need low-cost spay or neuter services, Contra Costa County Animal Services and local clinics are good places to start. Have questions about a community cat? Email us and we will point you in the right direction.

Found a Stray Cat or Kitten?

If you find a cat that seems lost or a litter of kittens, observe from a distance first — many community cats have caretakers, and very young kittens often have a mother nearby who has stepped away. If the cat is sick, injured, or in danger, contact local animal services. If you would like guidance, reach out to us at dirtypawsanimalrescue@gmail.com.

Welcoming a New Cat Home

Give a newly adopted cat a quiet room to start, with food, water, a litter box, and a cozy hiding spot. Let them explore at their own pace and introduce other pets slowly. A little patience in the first few weeks goes a long way toward a happy, lasting bond.
